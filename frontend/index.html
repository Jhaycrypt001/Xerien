How it works

An agent, not a chatbot.

01

Plan

Scout breaks your question into angles to investigate and decides what evidence would answer it.

02

Search & read

It runs several web searches and opens the best pages in full. It keeps going until the evidence holds up, then stops.

03

Report

You get a TL;DR, cited findings, analysis, risks, a 0–100 confidence score and three follow-up questions.
