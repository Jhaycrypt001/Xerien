How it works

An agent, not a chatbot.

01

Pull the numbers

Scout spots the tokens, contract addresses and protocols in your question and pulls their live price, liquidity and TVL before it starts.

02

Search & read

It plans its research, runs its own web searches and reads the best pages. It stops once the evidence holds up.

03

Report

You get a TL;DR, cited findings, analysis, risks, a 0–100 confidence score and three follow-up questions.

Features

Numbers first, then the narrative.

Live market data

Mention $ETH, $SOL, a contract address on any chain or a protocol like Aave, and the report starts from live DexScreener and DefiLlama numbers, which the agent then checks against the news.

Scan your wallet

Sign in with Phantom, MetaMask or any Solana or EVM wallet. Scout reads your holdings across eight chains, prices them and researches the risks of each position.

Cited, with a confidence score

Every finding links to its source, and the live trace shows each search and page read. Scout scores its own confidence and lists what it doesn't know.

History and share links

Every report is saved to your wallet's history. Each one has a public link you can send to anyone, and only you can delete it.

Security

Your keys stay yours.

  • Signature only. Sign-in is a free message signature. Scout never requests a transaction or an approval.
  • Read-only data. Wallet scans read public balances. Nothing is moved or written on-chain.
  • Locked-down sessions. Single-use, expiring sign-in challenges and hashed sessions in HttpOnly cookies.
  • Untrusted data stays data. Market data is sanitized and passed to the model as data, never as instructions.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Scout ever touch my funds?

No. Signing in is a message signature, which costs nothing and can't move funds. Scout never asks for a transaction or token approval.

Which wallets can I use?

Any Solana wallet that supports the Wallet Standard (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and more) and any EVM wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet and more).

Which chains are supported?

Wallet scans cover Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Optimism and Polygon. Research and token lookups work for any chain DexScreener and DefiLlama track.

Which AI model runs the research?

Scout runs on Google Gemini with Google Search grounding, or on Anthropic's Claude with web search and page reading, depending on how it's deployed.

What's the difference between Quick and Deep?

Quick runs a few searches and returns faster. Deep runs more searches and reads more pages, so it takes longer and goes further.

Is it free?

Yes. Each wallet can start up to 20 research runs per hour.

Is this financial advice?

No. Scout is a research tool: it gathers evidence, cites it and tells you how confident it is. Decisions are yours.
