How it works

An agent, not a chatbot.

01

Pull the numbers

Scout spots the tokens, contract addresses and protocols in your question and pulls their live price, liquidity and TVL before it starts.

02

Search & read

It plans its research, runs its own web searches and reads the best pages. It stops once the evidence holds up.

03

Report

You get a TL;DR, cited findings, analysis, risks, a 0–100 confidence score and three follow-up questions.

Features

Numbers first, then the narrative.

Live market data

Mention $ETH, $SOL, a contract address on any chain or a protocol like Aave, and the report starts from live DexScreener and DefiLlama numbers, which the agent then checks against the news.

Scan your wallet

Sign in with Phantom, MetaMask or any Solana or EVM wallet. Scout reads your holdings on Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Optimism and Polygon, prices them and researches the risks of each position. It never asks for a transaction.

Cited, with a confidence score

Every finding links to its source, and the live trace shows each search and page read. Scout scores its own confidence and lists what it doesn't know.

Your wallet is your account

No email or password. Reports are saved to your wallet's history, and each one has a link you can share.

Ask your first question.

Sign in with Phantom, Solflare, Backpack or MetaMask. No email, no password.
